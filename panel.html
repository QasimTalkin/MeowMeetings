iCal Sync Guide

  1. Open Google Calendar in your web browser.
  2. Hover over your primary calendar in the left sidebar, click the three dots, and select Settings and sharing.
  3. Scroll down to the bottom to the Integrate calendar section.
  4. Copy the Secret address in iCal format link.
  5. Paste it here! It works 100% locally and privately.
Warning: Keep this URL secret as it permits calendar viewing.
